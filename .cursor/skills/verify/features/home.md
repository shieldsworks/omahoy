# Home page

The front page of omahoy.org: the OMAHOY wordmark, a dot chart of San
Francisco Bay with Dash at her berth and three sample AIS vessels moving on
it, and the list of apps, each linking to its own page or its repo.

## Sub-features

- `home-render` the page paints with the wordmark over the Bay chart
- `home-traffic` the chart's status bar counts the sample vessels and names the nearest one
- `home-apps` the app list links every app page

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open `https://omahoy.org/`. Locally, `$base/`.
- The `omahoy.org` link at the top left of any app page.

## Driving it with headless Chromium

Preconditions:

- The baseline in `README.md`: Launch and Doctor pass, with a fresh `$run/profile`.

- **Render.** Open the home page. Run `drive home-render "$base/"`. `page.png` shows the wordmark over the chart. `dom.html` contains `<svg class="wordmark"` and `<canvas id="bay"`.
- **Traffic.** Same run. `grep -o '<span id="ais">[^<]*' "$run/artifacts/verify/home-render/dom.html"` reads `AIS 3`, and `grep -o 'id="nearest">[^<]*'` on the same file reads `Nearest <vessel> <n> nm`.
- **Apps.** Same run. `diff <(ls site/*/index.html | cut -d/ -f2) <(grep -o 'href="[a-z]*/"' "$run/artifacts/verify/home-render/dom.html" | cut -d'"' -f2 | tr -d / | sort)` prints nothing: the list links each `site/<app>/` page once.

## Gotchas

- `bay.js` is generated by `scripts/site-data.py`. A chart that looks wrong is a regeneration question, not a page edit.
- The chart animates unless reduced motion is on, so two screenshots differ in where the vessels are, and the nearest vessel changes. Compare `dom.html` handles, not pixels.
- Headless Chrome 154 reports a light color scheme, so the home page paints in `rose-pine`, not `tokyo-night` (measured). Read `data-theme` before judging colors.
